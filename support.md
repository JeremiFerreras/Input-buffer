# Support and bug reports

For help or to report a problem, open an issue in the [GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/JeremiFerreras/Input-buffer/issues).

A useful report includes:

- Geometry Dash version and platform.
- Geode version and Predictive Input Buffer version.
- Active prediction stage and gameplay mode.
- Whether Click Between Frames, TPS tools, or other input/physics mods are enabled.
- The level and approximate percentage where the problem occurred.
- The exact console message and the steps needed to reproduce the behavior.

For prediction issues, say whether the input was passed through, reported as `SAVED +N TICKS`, or passed as `RAW`, and what happened in the game afterward. A short video is especially useful for timing problems.

Please remove account identifiers and other personal information from logs or screenshots before posting them.
